# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in FrigateSpotter welcoming, respectful, and productive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about technical or safety concerns.
- Respect privacy and do not post credentials, private camera feeds, personal data, or other sensitive information.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, moderation, and project direction.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate intimidation or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code, credential-stealing behavior, or unsafe instructions intended to compromise users or systems.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this policy. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Reports should be made privately to the repository owner or maintainers through an available private GitHub contact or security-reporting mechanism. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and avoid unnecessary disclosure of reporter information.

## Scope

This policy applies in project-managed spaces and when someone is representing the project in public community spaces.
